# The Black and White

This folder houses source material for the contemporary criminal-cases desk.

## Folder layout

- `articles/` - article metadata and research notes, not copied articles
- `court-records/` - public indictments, docket sheets, opinions, and filings
- `images/` - lawfully obtained headshots and court images with rights metadata
- `pdfs/` - downloaded public documents with source URLs and access dates
- `metadata/` - manifests, citations, and verification notes

## Editorial standard

Each case entry separates allegations, charges, procedural posture, verdicts, appeals, and unresolved questions. Link to the original publisher or court source. Do not redistribute copyrighted articles or images without permission.
