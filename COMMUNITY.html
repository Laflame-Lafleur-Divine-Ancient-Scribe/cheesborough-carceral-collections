Why the official ledgers don't match the cemetery: When state officials were questioned in 1969 about deaths at the Arthur G. Dozier School for Boys, they produced an official ledger claiming fewer than 31 boys had died over seventy years. They swore all were accounted for in county records.

The Reality Behind the Bureaucratic Silence: The authority was hiding things. USF forensic anthropologists uncovered at least 55 unmarked burial shafts in the woods of Boot Hill. Boys beaten to death in the “White House” were buried in clandestine pine boxes without state death certificates, while intake ledgers were altered or skipped to cover the tracks of the superintendent and guards.

We are cross-referencing local Marianna funeral home receipts from 1928–1944 against the state intake rolls. The discrepancies prove systematic falsification. #DozierCoverup #MissingLedgers #BootHillForensics

Advice for families: Don't give up when DOC claims “No records found.”

When we filed a public records request for my brother's disciplinary transfer papers after he was moved out of Region 2, the classification clerk sent back a form letter saying the records were “lost during an office relocation.”

How We Overcame the Administrative Wall: We used the Clerk of Circuit Court docket in Bradford County and requested the judicial transport order rather than asking the prison. The judicial order proved he was transferred the exact day after filing a medical grievance. The state always tries to hide retaliation behind administrative disorganization.

If anyone needs the exact public records statutory template we used under Florida Chapter 119, let me know below. #FloridaPrisons #FamilyCircle

Yesterday • Jacksonville, FL • 🌐 Federal Practice Note

Spoliation of Evidence in Carceral Civil Rights Litigation:

In 42 U.S.C. § 1983 litigation, defense counsel routinely asserts that plaintiffs lack contemporaneous documentation of solitary confinement conditions or cell block temperatures. But when that absence is caused by the facility destroying shift logs or overwriting surveillance footage after receiving a litigation hold notice, the law provides a remedy.

Federal Precedent on Concealment: Under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 37(e) and 11th Circuit doctrine, when authorities deliberately conceal or fail to preserve electronically stored records with an intent to deprive, the court may instruct the jury that the lost information was unfavorable to the state. Concealment is not an immunity shield; it is an adverse inference.

Check the Habeas and Evidence sections in our Law Library for sample motions on adverse inference sanctions. #8thAmendment #LegalRights

Archival Truth • Our Principle
“The official record never tells the whole story.”

State ledgers, institutional reports, and administrative files often reflect the priorities of the agencies that created them. They can leave out abuse, conflict, resistance, family testimony, and the experiences of people whose lives were reduced to a line in a ledger.

The Yard brings families, historians, researchers, and advocates together to question those records, compare them against other evidence, recover missing voices, and examine what the official version may have overlooked, minimized, or left unexplained.
