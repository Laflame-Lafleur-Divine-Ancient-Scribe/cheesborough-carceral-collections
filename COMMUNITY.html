Stop letting 12 pull you over and hold you on the curb for a drug dog:

Under Rodriguez v. United States, the Supreme Court already ruled cops cannot drag out a routine traffic stop just to wait on a K-9 dog unit to sniff around your wheels. Once they write the ticket or run your license, the stop is officially over. Period.

How to Catch 'Em on Bodycam Discovery: When cops know they got no probable cause, they start asking 50 questions about where you coming from, what's in the trunk, or stall at their cruiser pretending the computer is slow. Subpoena that dashcam timestamp and CAD dispatch log. If they held you 10 extra minutes waiting on the dog, file a motion to suppress and throw the evidence in the trash!

Know the paperwork so you don't get railroaded into a bid. #RodriguezRule #MotionToSuppress #KnowYourRights

Archival Truth • Our Principle
“The official record never tells the whole story.”

State ledgers, institutional reports, and administrative files often reflect the priorities of the agencies that created them. They can leave out abuse, conflict, resistance, family testimony, and the experiences of people whose lives were reduced to a line in a ledger.

The Yard brings families, historians, researchers, and advocates together to question those records, compare them against other evidence, recover missing voices, and examine what the official version may have overlooked, minimized, or left unexplained.
