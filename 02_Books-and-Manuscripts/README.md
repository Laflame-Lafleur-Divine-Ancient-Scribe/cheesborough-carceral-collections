# Books and Manuscripts

- `Books/01_Primary-Texts`: books written during or close to the period being studied.
- `Books/02_Secondary-Scholarship`: academic books and monographs.
- `Books/03_Reference`: dictionaries, bibliographies, finding aids, and general reference works.
- `Manuscripts/01_Originals`: original-born digital documents or originals awaiting preservation.
- `Manuscripts/02_Digital-Scans`: scans and image-based reproductions.
- `Manuscripts/03_Transcriptions`: typed transcriptions and OCR-cleaned text.
- `99_To-Review`: material that needs identification, naming, or rights review.
