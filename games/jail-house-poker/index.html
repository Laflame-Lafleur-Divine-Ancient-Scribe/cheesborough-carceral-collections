Your chips are used only inside Jail House Poker. They cannot be purchased, cashed out, transferred for real money, or exchanged for prizes.

Cards are dealt at random. Your decisions, your opponent’s tendencies, and the way each hand develops determine what happens at the table. No hand is guaranteed.
