What the website records

Carceral Collections uses first-party analytics to understand which pages people read, how they find the site, and whether the site works well. The owner dashboard uses these records to report website activity.

  • Anonymous visits: the browser stores a randomly generated visitor identifier. A session groups activity until 30 minutes of inactivity. These identifiers help distinguish new and returning visits without a device fingerprint.
  • Pages and discovery: records include page paths, the referring website's host, and campaign tags such as UTM parameters. Page URL query strings are removed, except the public video identifier used by the video watch page.
  • Reading activity: engagement time is counted while the page is visible, focused, and not idle. The system can also record how far a reader scrolls.
  • Technology: broad browser, operating-system, and device categories help explain how the site is used.

Approximate location

When configured and enabled, a server-side MaxMind lookup can estimate a visitor's country and region, and optionally city. Location is approximate and can be affected by a VPN, proxy, or network provider. The analytics record does not retain the raw IP address, collect street-level location, or create a device fingerprint. When location is unavailable, the dashboard shows that the data is missing.

Signed-in members

The existing account session cookie can associate recorded activity with a signed-in member. Private account and activity information is available only through the protected owner dashboard. Administrative actions also use the site's existing audit record.

How long records are kept

Analytics retention defaults to 365 days and can be set by the owner from 1 to 730 days. Security retention defaults to 30 days. The owner can change retention and collection settings in the private dashboard.

Exclusions and browser signals

The collector honors supported Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Owner dashboard activity is excluded. Collection settings also allow the owner to exclude owner visits, recognized bots, and development traffic.

The checkbox above saves the ccc-analytics-excluded preference in browser storage. When it is enabled, that browser is excluded from analytics. This does not sign you out or remove your account.
