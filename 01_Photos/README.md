# Photo Archive

Use `01_By-Collection` as the primary storage location for website photography.

- `Historic` and `Contemporary` are the time period.
- `Men`, `Women`, and `Juveniles` are the subject collection.
- Use `02_By-State` when the state or location is the primary research question.
- Place unidentified or not-yet-reviewed files in `02_By-State/00_Unassigned` or `05_Archive-Admin/Incoming/00_To-Sort`.

Keep the original filename when possible. Add date, location, and rights information in the research metadata record rather than renaming files repeatedly.
