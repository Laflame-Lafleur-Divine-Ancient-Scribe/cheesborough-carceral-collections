Carceral Collections • Assistance Service

Research Help Finder

Finding the right information can be difficult, especially when trying to help someone who is incarcerated. Carceral Collections created the Research Help Finder to give incarcerated individuals, families, friends, advocates, and researchers a place to explain what they are looking for and receive assistance identifying potentially useful records, documents, correspondence, research materials, and public resources.
