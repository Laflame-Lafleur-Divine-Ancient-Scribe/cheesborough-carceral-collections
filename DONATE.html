Support the preservation of the record.

The Cheesborough Carceral Collections is developing an open, carefully described repository for photographs, legal materials, manuscripts, and personal histories used by researchers and communities.

Why independent support matters

Archives shape what can be remembered, questioned, and taught. Independent support allows this collection to develop with scholarly care rather than being narrowed by commercial priorities or short funding cycles.

Your contribution helps preserve vulnerable materials, improve their descriptions, and make them more discoverable to students, graduate researchers, advocates, families, and the public.

What your support makes possible

  • Digitizing photographs, manuscripts, and fragile primary sources.
  • Creating accurate metadata, finding aids, and research guides.
  • Maintaining public access to an independently organized collection.
  • Supporting scholarship that approaches carceral history with context and care.
