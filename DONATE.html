Turning Hidden History into Real Change

Real reform starts with knowing the truth. The history of adult prisons and youth detention is often buried in old courthouse basements, locked files, and forgotten archives. We dig up these records and make them easy to find so that families, researchers, and community leaders can see the evidence for themselves.

Your support helps pay for record fees, scanning tools, travel, and website costs. Most importantly, it ensures that people who were caught up in these systems are remembered as human beings—not just a case number on a piece of paper.

What Your Support Makes Possible

  • Unlocking Records: Paying government fees to get hidden court papers and state files opened to the public.
  • Saving Family Stories: Carefully scanning and saving photos, letters, and personal histories before they are lost.
  • Supporting Reform: Helping people understand the history of adult and youth prisons so we can build fairer policies today.
  • Free Public Access: Keeping our online search tools and document library free for everyone to read and use.
