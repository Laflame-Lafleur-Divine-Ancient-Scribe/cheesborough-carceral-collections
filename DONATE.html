Carceral Collections follows the paper trail: court files, photographs, prison records, newspapers, family materials, and the stories tied to them.

Help keep the digging going

Good records do not always come easy. They are tucked into courthouse files, old newspapers, family collections, archives, and places most people never get a chance to see. This work brings them into view.

Support helps cover record requests, research trips, scanning, document care, website upkeep, and the long hours it takes to connect the dots. It helps make sure the people inside these stories are not reduced to a case number or left out of the record.

What your support makes possible

  • More public records, hard-to-find documents, and overlooked leads.
  • Careful scanning and preservation for photographs, files, and family history.
  • Stories that give context to the people, places, and choices behind the paperwork.
  • A public home for readers who want to look at the evidence for themselves.
