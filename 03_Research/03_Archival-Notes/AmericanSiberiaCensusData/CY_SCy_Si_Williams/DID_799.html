             Friday April 9th A.D. 1869

Court met pursuant to adjournment Criminal Docket 

Continued



State of Florida     } Receiving                }      Solicitor & Arnow}

      vs             } Stolen Good Knowing them }   

James E. Collins     } to have been Stolen      }      Gardener & Finley}  

            On this day came the Solicitor Archibald T. Banks who

prosecutes the pleas of the State in this behalf as well as the prisoner 

in person who having entered a plea of not Guilty thereupon came a 

Jury to wit

1  Henry Thomas         5  Butler Chisholm           9   Isaac Peach

2  Walter Price         6  Benjamin Welcome          10  London Addison

3  Christopher Cummings 7  George Williams           11  Gilbert Jones

4  William Nelson       8  Arthur Simmons            12  Thomas Scott

who having been duly sworn well and truly to try and the

deliberations made between the State of Florida and the prisoner 

at the Bar after hearing the testimony of the witnesses the 

argument of Council and the charge of the Court retired in 

charge of the Court retired in charge of the Baliff

to consider on their verdict and afterwards to wit on the 

same day returned into Court and rendered the 

following verdict, We the Jury find the defendant 

Guilty, thereupon it is ordered Considered and

and adjudged by the Court that the said James E. Collins

be sentenced to three years imprisonment at hard labour

in the peniteniary for the term of Three years



        The Jury was called in open court and each individually

announced guilty as his verdict in the above entitled

cause
