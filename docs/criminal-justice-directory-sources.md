# Criminal Justice Directory source checks

Reviewed September 8, 2026. The supplied list contained 73 rows and 72 unique organizations; the repeated Southern Center for Human Rights row is combined. A checkedDate records review of an official source, not confirmation of active intake, representation, or all services. Types indicate the primary directory role, not every activity.

## Significant corrections

- California Innocence Project is now [California Western Innocence and Justice Clinic](https://www.cwsl.edu/experiential_learning/clinics/california_western_innocence_and_justice_clinic/index.html). Its current intake is limited to selected Southern California jurisdictions.
- [Youth First](https://www.nokidsinprison.org/) uses Youth First Justice Collaborative; [NYJN](https://nyjn.org/about-us/) uses National Youth Justice Network.
- [JPI closure notice](https://justicepolicy.org/press/justice-doesnt-end-here-jpi-to-close-after-nearly-30-years-of-impact/): operations sunset August 15, 2025; research remains accessible.
- [PJI reduced operations notice](https://www.pretrial.org/insights/blog/2025/11/18/under-attack-but-not-defeated-honoring-our-legacy-with-a-generative-pause): November 2025 generative pause; resources stay accessible.
- NCADP: previously supplied ncadp.org displayed unrelated casino navigation during review. No trustworthy current official site confirmed. Retained as a flagged entry with no outbound URL.
- [Root & Rebound support](https://www.rootandreboundsc.org/get-support/): old domain redirects to the South Carolina domain, but official pages still list California and South Carolina support. Availability must be checked.
- [FRRC fines and fees](https://floridarrc.com/fines-and-fees-assistance-program/): applications paused when checked; directory does not promise reopening.
- [Georgia Justice Project](https://www.gjp.org/get-help/): defense in Fulton/DeKalb counties; records assistance for Georgia arrests.
- Florida Justice Institute redirects to fji.law; NAPD to thenapd.org; Louisiana Center for Children's Rights to lakidsrights.org; VOTE to voiceoftheexperienced.org.
- LAC and National HIRE Network official pages required JavaScript and did not expose substantive current service details to the checking tool. No checkedDate or direct intake link is assigned.
