The Dozier School Newspaper Collection

A digitized chronicle of The Yellow Jacket, the official bi-weekly student newspaper printed by youth in the print shop of the Florida Industrial School for Boys at Marianna from 1933 through 1960.

About The Yellow Jacket Archive (1933–1960)

Produced on campus under the direction of trade instructors and school superintendents, The Yellow Jacket offers an extraordinary primary record of institutional life. It documents varsity athletic schedules, campus construction, holiday festivities, vocational shop work, and administrative announcements. Read critically alongside forensic findings and survivor testimony, these newspapers illuminate how the state constructed public normalcy while concealing institutional violence.
